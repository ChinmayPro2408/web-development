Matheran

Matheran is Asia's only automobile-free hill station, celebrated for its pristine, pollution-free air, vibrant red laterite mud paths, and colonial-era charm. Located in the Western Ghats of Maharashtra at an elevation of approximately 800 metres (2,625 feet), its name beautifully translates to "forest on the forehead" of the mountains in Marathi. Because the Ministry of Environment has designated it as an eco-sensitive zone, all motorized vehicles are strictly banned past the Dasturi Naka entry point, leaving the town to be explored entirely by foot, horse, or hand-pulled rickshaws.
